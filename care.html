Care Instructions

Keep your handmade pieces looking beautiful for years to come

💧

Moisture

Keep away from excessive moisture and direct sunlight

🪥

Dusting

Dust gently with a soft, dry cloth when needed

🫧

Washing

📦

Storage

🤍

Handling

Handle with care to ensure long-lasting beauty

To keep your handmade crochet creations looking beautiful:

  • Keep away from excessive moisture and direct sunlight.
  • Dust gently with a soft, dry cloth when needed.
  • Hand wash only if necessary; do not machine wash.
  • Store in a clean, dry place to maintain shape and quality.
  • Handle with care to ensure long-lasting beauty.
